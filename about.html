About the Show

Three Men. Anything & Everything.

The Rivers Die Podcast is exactly what it sounds like — three guys sitting down to talk about anything and everything. UFOs and Area 51 lore, technology and Starlink, pop culture and celebrity roasts, half-baked theories, and the kind of tangents that only happen between family.

Brothers Joe and Michael teamed up with their cousin Justin to record unfiltered, free-ranging conversations. New episodes are available on YouTube, Spotify, and Apple Podcasts.
